I'm a PhD student at Stanford. I earned my bachelor's degree at the University of Wisconsin-Madison and my master's in computer science at Stanford.

My research focuses on computer graphics, neural rendering, and systems engineering. I’m working on ways to bring representations, algorithms, and systems engineering practices from traditional graphics to neural graphics and diffusion models. I believe the future of graphics and image/video generation won't be fully learned or fully rasterized/ray-traced. I see hybrid architectures as a way to improve representation learning, efficiency, and scalability.
